4. Privacy and Data Protection

The University of Basel processes personal data in accordance with the Information and Data Protection Act of the Canton of Basel-Stadt (IDG Basel-Stadt, SG 153.260), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/DSG), and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data processed

  • Account details: Matrix user ID (e.g. @username:matrix.dmi.unibas.ch), display name, and the attributes your identity provider transmits at sign-in (such as name and email address, as released by Switch edu-ID).
  • Connection and protocol data: IP addresses, timestamps, device names and session identifiers, room membership events, presence and read receipts, and error logs. This data is needed for technical operation, security, and abuse handling. Logs are kept only as long as these purposes require.
  • Message content: In end-to-end encrypted rooms, messages, attachments, and calls are encrypted on your devices and only room members hold the keys; server operators cannot read this content. Room settings determine whether a room is encrypted. In unencrypted rooms, message events are stored on the server and are technically accessible to server administrators.
  • No federation: This server is not connected to other Matrix servers. Your messages are not forwarded outside this service.
  • Push notifications: If you enable push notifications in a mobile app, notification metadata is delivered through the app vendor's push gateway and Apple or Google. Message content of encrypted rooms is not included.

Retention and backups

  • Your responsibility: In accordance with ANBITS, you are responsible for archiving and backing up important academic data, research materials, and documents yourself.
  • System backups: Server backups may be made for disaster recovery and operational continuity. They are not an individual archive service, and individual messages or files cannot be restored on request.
  • Media: Uploaded media and caches may be removed automatically under retention limits.
